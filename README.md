## 📂 Projects

### [CreditWise: Loan Approval Prediction](https://github.com/pruthvicodes/Creditwise_Loan_Approval)
Predicts whether a loan application should be approved from income, credit score, debts and collateral. Compares Logistic Regression, KNN and Naive Bayes with GridSearchCV tuning. **86.5% accuracy**, plus a ready-to-use prediction script.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `pandas` `Seaborn`

### [Heart Disease Prediction](https://github.com/pruthvicodes/Heart-disease-prediction)
Predicts heart disease from 13 clinical measurements (UCI dataset, 303 patients). Full exploratory analysis and Logistic Regression with **86.8% test accuracy and 90% recall** on heart-disease cases, with no overfitting.
`Python` `scikit-learn` `pandas` `Matplotlib`

### CapacityIQ: ED Operations Intelligence *(final-year project, in progress)*
A seven-module decision-support system for emergency departments: patient-volume forecasting (Prophet vs SARIMA), admission prediction (AUC 0.84 on 560k visits), rule-based bed allocation, staffing estimation, utilisation analytics, SHAP explanations and an LLM-written briefing, all in a FastAPI + Streamlit dashboard with what-if analysis.
`Prophet` `scikit-learn` `SHAP` `FastAPI` `Streamlit` `Gemini API`
