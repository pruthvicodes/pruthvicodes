<h2 align="center">👨‍💻 About Me</h2>

I'm a final-year **B.E. student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** at Dr. D. Y. Patil Institute of Engineering, Management & Research, Pune.

I enjoy taking a messy real-world dataset all the way to something useful: cleaning it, finding the patterns, training and comparing models, **explaining why a model made its prediction**, and wrapping it in a tool people can actually use.

- 🔭 **Working on:** CapacityIQ, an AI decision-support system that helps hospital emergency departments plan beds and staff (final-year team project)
- 🌱 **Learning:** explainable AI (SHAP), LLM integration and deploying ML apps
- 🎯 **Looking for:** internships and entry-level roles in Data Science / Machine Learning
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I like checking a model's results twice, especially when they look too good to be true
